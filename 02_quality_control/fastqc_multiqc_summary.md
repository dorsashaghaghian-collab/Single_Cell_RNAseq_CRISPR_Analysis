# Quality Control Summary

FastQC was performed on raw sequencing reads.

MultiQC was used to combine results from all samples.

Evaluated metrics:

- Read quality
- GC content
- Sequence duplication
- Adapter contamination
- Overrepresented sequences

Results:

All samples showed consistent quality profiles.

Average read length:
91 bp

GC content:
~49%

No major adapter contamination was detected.
